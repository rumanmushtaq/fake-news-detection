How it works

From pasted text to a verdict in four steps

The same pipeline runs every time you click Analyze. Nothing is sent to a third-party API.

  1. STEP 01 {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="file" %}

    Paste the story

    A headline, a social post or a full article. The model sees the first 512 tokens, about 390 words.

    "BRUSSELS (Reuters) - European Union finance ministers…"
  2. STEP 02 {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="tokens" %}

    Tokenize

    WordPiece breaks the text into sub-word tokens from a 30,522-entry vocabulary.

    [CLS] brussels ( reuters ) - european … [SEP]
  3. STEP 03 {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="layers" %}

    Read in context

    12 transformer layers weigh every token against every other one, picking up tone, structure and wording.

    12 layers × 12 heads × 768 dims
  4. STEP 04 {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="gauge" %}

    Verdict + confidence

    A classifier head scores Fake vs Real. You get the label, both probabilities and a saved record.

    softmax → P(fake), P(real)

Under the hood

A transformer that reads style, not just headlines

BERT reads the whole passage at once, in both directions. That's how it picks up the sensational framing, loaded wording and missing attribution that separate the two kinds of writing in its training data.

01

WordPiece tokenizer

Lower-cases the text and splits rare words into known pieces, so no word is ever unknown.

02

bert-base-uncased encoder

12 layers, 768-dimensional hidden states, about 110 million parameters.

03

Classification head

Maps the [CLS] summary vector to two scores, turned into probabilities with softmax.
