How to read this result

The model compares the style of your text (tone, framing, wording, attribution) with the labeled real and fake articles it was trained on.

It does not look up facts or sources. A carefully written false story can read as real, and an unusual real one can read as fake.

Its training data is mostly US political news from 2016–2017, so it's less reliable on other topics, periods and languages.

What confidence means

Confidence is the probability the model assigns to its chosen label. Anything under {{ low_confidence_pct }}% is flagged as low confidence.

High confidence doesn't mean the verdict is right, only that the text looks very typical of one class.
